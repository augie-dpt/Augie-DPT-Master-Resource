What this is

A complete set of study notes for the Augustana DPT first-year curriculum, written by a student who took these exact courses the year before you. Every module page is built from that module's lectures, slide decks and transcripts, arranged in the order the course teaches them.

How to use it

  • Read them while the lecture plays. Each module page runs in the same order the lecture is delivered, so the topic banners line up with wherever you are in the recording. Open the recording from your own Canvas module — they are cohort-specific.
  • Annotate as you go. These are a scaffold, not a transcript. Add what the instructor says off-slide, your own examples, the thing that finally made it click. The students who get the most out of them are the ones whose copy stops looking like the original.
  • The callouts carry the signal. 🎙 marks something the instructor said out loud that isn't on the slide. ★ marks something exam-relevant or something you were told to prepare.
  • Every topic ends with a glossary. Cover the right column and work down it — that is a complete self-quiz in about ninety seconds.
  • Figures come from the real decks. Click any figure to open it full-size.

What is NOT here

  • Lecture recordings, Panopto and Zoom links — those are tied to your cohort. Open them from your own Canvas.
  • Textbook chapters. Readings are cited by chapter and page; get the book through the library or VitalSource.
  • Anything with a due date. Assignment deadlines, exam times and room assignments change every year — Canvas is the source of truth.
★
If a note and Canvas ever disagree, Canvas wins. Course content gets revised between cohorts. Tell whoever maintains this and it will get fixed.
